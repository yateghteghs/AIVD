In short
  • AIVD gives general information from the Electoral Act 2026 and INEC guidance. It is not legal advice.

  • It will not tell you who to vote for, predict results, or help anyone interfere with an election.

About AI Voter Desk

AI Voter Desk (AIVD) is a prototype built by Index Prima for the Rise Networks AI4Elections hackathon. It is not run by, endorsed by or affiliated with the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC). By using AIVD you agree to these terms.

Information, not legal advice

AIVD answers by quoting and citing the Electoral Act 2026 and documents published by INEC. It is general information to help you understand the rules, not legal advice about your situation. For a dispute or a decision that matters to you, speak to INEC or a qualified lawyer.

Accuracy

  • We work to quote official texts correctly, but AIVD can be wrong or incomplete. Some INEC documents were scanned, and their text was read by software; AIVD labels that text until it has been checked.
  • Older INEC documents (for example from the 2023 election) are labelled "dated" and may no longer apply.
  • When the sources don't clearly answer a question, AIVD says "Not covered" instead of guessing.
  • Election dates, polling units and results come only from INEC. Always check INEC's official channels.

If you spot a mistake, please use "Flag for review" on the answer.

Neutrality

AIVD does not tell anyone who to vote for, does not compare or rate parties or candidates, and does not predict outcomes. It treats every party and candidate the same.

Acceptable use

You agree not to use AIVD to:

  • plan or promote vote buying, rigging, intimidation, impersonation or any other electoral offence;
  • create or spread false information about elections, including presenting AIVD's answers as official INEC statements;
  • overload, attack or scrape the service, or try to get around its safeguards;
  • submit other people's personal information.

Your ratings and flags

When you rate an answer or flag it for review, you allow the AIVD team to use it to improve the service. How this information is handled is explained in the privacy notice.

Sources

The Electoral Act 2026 and INEC documents quoted by AIVD are public documents published by INEC at inecnigeria.org. Each answer links to the document and page it quotes.
